I'm a Staff Software Engineer focused on building ambitious ideas into polished mobile and desktop products. [`TypeScript`](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) is my primary language, with [`React`](https://react.dev/) for UI and [`Node.js`](https://nodejs.org/) for development tooling and services. When a project calls for native, deeper system access, or performance, I work with [`Rust`](https://www.rust-lang.org/) and [`Tauri`](https://tauri.app/) or [`C++`](https://isocpp.org/) and [`JUCE`](https://juce.com/).

I work remotely from my home on a small lake in [`Texas`](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas) for a large [`NYC`](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City)-based [`D2C`](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-to-consumer) conglomerate, building [`headless`](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headless_commerce) commerce systems with [`TypeScript`](https://www.typescriptlang.org/), [`React Router`](https://reactrouter.com/), and [`Shopify`](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless) across its portfolio of brands.

Tech, music, and motor vehicles are my passions. In software, that curiosity draws me to on-device AI, encrypted [`peer-to-peer`](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-to-peer) systems, self-hosted and [`local-first`](https://www.inkandswitch.com/local-first/) apps built to outlive their servers, and audio/music tools.
